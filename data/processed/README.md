# data/processed: what each file is

**Licence**: CC BY 4.0 (attribution: Jasco John; code is MIT, the report and figures CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; see `LICENSE`). The raw data are not covered and are not redistributed.

Everything here is an **aggregate** written by the pipeline (no respondent-level rows; a cell with fewer than 10 unweighted respondents carries no estimate: `docs/data_terms.md`). These files let a person without the
restricted raw data run `python run_all.py --from-processed`. They are regenerated by `python run_all.py --from-raw` (the folder is emptied by `--clean`).

Type: **COMPUTED** = calculated from survey microdata by a script here; **CITED** = published figures transcribed with their source and page; **CALIBRATED** = a computed fit to an observed target; **COMPUTED + CITED** = survey aggregates combined with statute.

| File | Contents | Source data | Written by | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `afrobarometer_morale.json` | Tax-morale components (civic duty, trust in the tax office, tax avoidance, reciprocity, difficulty of finding out about taxes) by level, with 95% CIs; the baseline scores of the tax-morale model; the civic-duty gaps | Afrobarometer Round 8, Tanzania (2021), weighted | `src/03_features/afrobarometer_morale.py` | COMPUTED |
| `agent_calibration.json` | The two calibrated constants of the agent simulation (`agents.conversion_rate`, `agents.p_comply.intercept`), the fit to TRA's registered-taxpayer trend, identifiability (correlation, profiles, the identified combination) and the record of the third-target search | `tra_registered_taxpayers.csv`, FinScope adult TIN rate | `src/04_models/agent_calibration.py` | CALIBRATED |
| `agent_cells.json` | Weighted cells of age band x mobile money x urban x propensity decile (each pooling at least 10 respondents) from which the simulated population is resampled | FinScope Tanzania 2023 and the targeting model | `src/04_models/targeting_model.py` | COMPUTED |
| `agent_income.json` | Percentiles of annual earnings by age band (and the national distribution) and the share of persons without earnings | ILFS 2024 | `src/03_features/agent_inputs.py` | COMPUTED |
| `agent_trust_morale.json` | Weighted distributions of trust in the tax office and of civic duty by age band x urban/rural | Afrobarometer Round 8 | `src/03_features/agent_inputs.py` | COMPUTED |
| `census_age_counts.json` | Population by single year of age and by lifecycle band (Seed 6-15: 16.35 million), 10% sample x 10 | Census 2022 10% sample | `src/03_features/census_population.py` | COMPUTED |
| `finscope_active_benchmark.json` | Region x activity benchmark cells for the Active-stage page (zone and national fall-backs; suppressed and low-precision cells flagged) | FinScope Tanzania 2023 | `src/03_features/finscope_active_benchmark_loader.py` | COMPUTED |
| `finscope_region_activity.json` | Region x activity prevalence and TIN rates (suppressed cells flagged) | FinScope Tanzania 2023 | `src/03_features/finscope_region_activity_loader.py` | COMPUTED |
| `hbs_turnover.json` | Turnover of non-farm household-business owners as aggregates: mean tax per owner (central: owners with receipts; low: all owners) of the Active and Enterprise groups, the share above TZS 4M and 11M, and the zone x activity and region x activity turnover bands behind Mzani wa Kodi (cells under 10 businesses suppressed; region cells need 30 with receipts); CPI-uprated to 2024 | HBS 2017/18 (NBS), Individual file, Consumption Aggregate, Form I S10C1; WDI FP.CPI.TOTL | `src/03_features/hbs_turnover.py` | COMPUTED |
| `tra_collections_2024_25.json` | TRA tax collections FY2024/25 as aggregates (individuals' income tax, PAYE, rental tax, domestic revenue, total gross; annual = sum of the four quarterly totals; million TZS) | TRA quarterly collections workbook FY2024/25 | `src/03_features/tra_collections.py` | CITED |
| `ilfs_indicators.json` | Informality, status in employment and enterprise registration shares (ICLS-17 and NBS-like definitions) | ILFS 2024 | `src/03_features/ilfs_indicators.py` | COMPUTED |
| `ilfs_tax_base.json` | Mean statutory tax per person of each Table 40 target group (with standard errors) and the age-earnings multiplier paths (5-year age bands), with the schedules of `config.yaml` applied | ILFS 2024 earnings x Income Tax Act schedules (Finance Act 2026 presumptive schedule) | `src/04_models/stage_economics.py` | COMPUTED + CITED |
| `tra_registered_taxpayers.csv` | TRA registered taxpayers (TIN registrations, Mainland, stock at the end of the fiscal year), 2011/12-2023/24, both vintages where the 2023/24 report restates 2022/23, with document, table and page | NBS Tax Statistics Reports 2022/23 and 2023/24 (source TRA) | `src/03_features/twin_inputs.py` | CITED |
| `twin_inputs.json` | The digital twin's base parameters with value, basis class and source (GDP growth, population, working-age population, registered taxpayers, adult population, FinScope adult mobile-money users, informal earnings, MSME receipts, the TRA-trend formalisation rate), the quantile cells of the earnings distributions the tax is applied to, the BoT account count as a context figure, the TCRA corroboration and the definitions | WDI snapshots, Census 2022, TRA/NBS, FinScope 2023, ILFS 2024, BoT, TCRA | `src/03_features/twin_inputs.py` | COMPUTED + CITED |

The World Bank WDI snapshots are in `data/raw/worldbank/` (tracked; CC BY 4.0), not here. The interim files (`data/interim/finscope_summary.json`, `finscope_feature_store.json`) are the FinScope aggregates written by `src/03_features/finscope_analysis.py`.
